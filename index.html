The Area

Montado country

The mill lies in the interior of Odemira, a municipality of about 1,700 km² that runs from rolling cork hills to roughly 55 km of Atlantic coast. Its entire coastline belongs to the Southwest Alentejo and Vicentine Coast Natural Park.

Around the house is the montado: open woodland of cork and holm oak, grazing sheep and quiet farm tracks. Alentejo holds most of Portugal's cork forest. The bark is stripped by hand about every nine years, and each trunk is painted with the number of its harvest year.

Things to do

Days out from the hill

Walk

Rota Vicentina

Around 750 km of marked trails. The inland Historical Way crosses cork woodland between Odemira and São Teotónio; the Fishermen's Trail follows the cliffs.

Swim

River pools & lakes

Pego das Pias, a rocky river pool near Odemira, or the river beach on the Santa Clara reservoir.

Coast

Wild beaches

Zambujeira do Mar, Carvalhal and Odeceixe for sand between cliffs. Cabo Sardão, the westernmost point of the Alentejo coast, for big sea views.

Surf

Atlantic waves

The Vicentine coast is one of Europe's best surf regions, with breaks and schools for every level.

Ride

Gravel & trail cycling

Quiet back roads and dirt tracks through oak country, with Rota Vicentina cycling routes passing Odemira.

Taste

Alentejo table

Black pork, slow-roasted lamb, octopus on the coast, local honey and medronho, the strawberry-tree spirit of the hills.
